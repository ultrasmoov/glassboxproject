The GlassBox Project
Nonpartisan · Nonprofit

Every campaign dollar, in public, in real time.

We give political campaigns a glass box: a bank account where every donation and every payment is published the moment it moves. Candidates who take the pledge have nothing to hide, and voters can see it for themselves.

Real time

Transactions appear within minutes, not in a filing months later.

Fair to small donors

Names stay private until someone gives more than $200 in a cycle, the same line the law draws.

Clean sources only

Money comes from real people or other glass boxes. Anything else goes back.
